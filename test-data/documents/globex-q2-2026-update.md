Globex operates a payments network for small online merchants. This update covers the quarter ended 30 June 2026.

## Quarter summary

Quarterly revenue was 1,340 million USD, up 9% year on year.

Total payment volume processed was 96 billion USD, up 14%.

Adjusted operating margin was 27%, down from 29% a year earlier.

## Why margin declined

The margin decline came from higher fraud losses and a one-time cost of 45 million USD to migrate data centres.

Fraud losses rose to 0.11% of payment volume from 0.08%, mostly from card-not-present transactions in two new markets.

## Merchant growth

Globex added 38,000 net new merchants in the quarter and ended with 612,000 active merchants.

Merchant churn fell to 1.9% per quarter from 2.3%.

## Cash and liquidity

Cash and short-term investments were 2,450 million USD at quarter end.

Globex has no outstanding bank loans. A 500 million USD credit line is undrawn.

## Guidance

Globex kept its full-year 2026 revenue growth guidance of 8% to 11%.

Management expects fraud losses to return below 0.09% of payment volume by the end of 2026 after new fraud models go live.
