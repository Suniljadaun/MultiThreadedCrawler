ACME Corp makes industrial robots and warehouse automation software. This report covers the fiscal year ended 31 December 2025.

## Financial highlights

Total revenue was 4,200 million USD, up 12% from 3,750 million USD in 2024.

Operating margin improved to 18.5% from 16.0%, mainly because software makes up a larger share of sales.

Net income was 610 million USD. Diluted earnings per share were 3.05 USD.

## Revenue drivers

The main revenue driver was the Robotics segment, which grew 15% on strong demand from logistics customers in Europe and Asia.

The second driver was recurring software subscriptions. Annual recurring revenue reached 900 million USD, up 28%, as more customers moved from one-time licences to subscriptions.

Spare parts and field service revenue was flat at 520 million USD.

## Segment results

### Robotics

Robotics revenue was 2,780 million USD. Segment operating margin was 14%.

### Software

Software revenue was 900 million USD with a segment operating margin of 35%.

### Services

Services revenue was 520 million USD with a segment operating margin of 11%.

## Risk factors

ACME depends on a small number of suppliers for precision motors. A disruption at one supplier could delay deliveries for several months.

About 40% of revenue comes from the ten largest customers, so losing a major customer would have a material effect.

Currency movements matter because 55% of sales are outside the United States.

## Capital allocation

ACME spent 380 million USD on research and development, equal to 9% of revenue.

The company paid a dividend of 0.80 USD per share and bought back 150 million USD of its own shares.

Net debt at year end was 1,100 million USD.

## Outlook for 2026

Management expects revenue growth of 8% to 10% in 2026, with software growing faster than hardware.

The company plans to open a new robot assembly plant in Poland in the second half of 2026.
