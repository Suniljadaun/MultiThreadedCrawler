Initech sells accounting software to mid-sized companies. This disclosure lists the main risks management has identified.

## Customer concentration

No single customer is more than 3% of revenue. The public sector makes up 22% of revenue, and government budget cuts could reduce renewals.

## Cybersecurity

Initech stores financial data for more than 9,000 customers. A breach could lead to regulatory fines, lawsuits and lost customers.

In 2025 Initech had one security incident. An attacker accessed a test environment that held no customer data.

## Key personnel

The company depends on a small team of senior engineers for its core ledger product. Losing several of them at once could slow product development.

## Legacy product migration

About 30% of customers still use the older on-premise product. Support for that product ends in March 2027, and customers who do not migrate may leave.

## Interest rates

Initech has a 200 million USD term loan with a floating interest rate. Each one percentage point rise in rates increases annual interest cost by about 2 million USD.
